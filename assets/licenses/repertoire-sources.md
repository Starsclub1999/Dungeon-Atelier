# Dungeon Atelier — additional real score sources

This bank contains **50 actual scores**, not generated pastiches. Downloaded `new-*.mid` files are byte-for-byte copies of the source MIDIs listed below. The Gregorian Dies irae MIDI is a new rhythmic realization of the complete CC0 GABC transcription; Scarborough Fair is a new MIDI transcription of the published traditional melody. Their GABC/LilyPond notation is bundled separately. SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `repertoire-manifest.json`. Rebuild offline with `node tools/build-repertoire.cjs`.

The studio preserves all melodic MIDI pitches, note lengths and velocities; tempo maps are flattened into fractional note positions by the existing MIDI importer. Playback tempo and instrument timbre are adapted. Scores shorter than 62 seconds at the selected tempo are repeated **in full**, never extended with random notes. Original channel-10 percussion is mapped to the six drum voices. Scores without channel-10 percussion receive a quiet, newly authored accompaniment; this is identified in each preset and is not presented as historical percussion.

Genre labels distinguish Renaissance/medieval scores, Gothic classical pieces, nocturnes, screen-related classics, actual retro-game music and licensed dungeon/RPG music. Freedoom is a free-content retro FPS project: these are its actual original MIDIs, not id Software’s proprietary Doom soundtrack. Fantasy Musica, Joseph Collard and Bogart VGM are credited as dungeon/RPG game composers, not falsely advertised as established dungeon-synth artists.

Film/game references identify reuse of the classical **composition**. The included Mutopia MIDI is not a transcription of the film/game recording or its later arrangement.

## Verified media connections

- **Bach · Menuet BWV 814 · Tetris C source**: The Menuet is the classical source of Tetris Type C. Original B-minor score; this is not Hirokazu Tanaka’s F-sharp-minor game arrangement. [Evidence](https://open.spotify.com/track/0gqnz9018kX0UmSDcMpPxK).
- **Bach · Toccata and Fugue in D minor · Fantasia**: The classical composition is heard in Fantasia, 20,000 Leagues Under the Sea and Gravity Falls. This preset uses the organ score, not the film arrangements. [Evidence](https://d23.com/fantasia-at-80-exploring-bach/).
- **Bach · Passacaglia and Fugue in C minor**: BWV 582 is referenced by the Walt Disney Archives in The Simpsons. This preset uses the complete organ score. [Evidence](https://d23.com/fantasia-at-80-exploring-bach/).
- **Beethoven · Moonlight Sonata I · Resident Evil**: The first movement is heard in Resident Evil’s mansion piano puzzle. This preset uses Beethoven’s complete first movement, not Capcom’s recording. [Evidence](https://game.capcom.com/residentevil/en/umbrella-20200821110000.html).
- **Mussorgsky · Night on Bald Mountain · Fantasia**: Night on Bald Mountain appears in Fantasia. This preset uses Konstantin Chernov’s piano arrangement, not the film recording or Stokowski arrangement. [Evidence](https://d23.com/a-to-z/fantasia-film/).
